Hi, I'm Xiao Xiao, and my project is called Find Your Next Game.
My question is: how can I find a Steam game that shares the features I
enjoy, runs on my computer, and fits my budget?
I'll follow one search. I liked Risk of Rain, I play on a Mac, and I have

0:35-1:15 | Starting game and data
【点 Edit search，指游戏标签，保留 Rogue-like、Indie、Platformer。
  点 Collapse setup。指快照日期和 57 candidates。】
These are the recorded tags for my starting game. I can choose the
features that matter to me. For this search, I'm keeping Rogue-like,
Indie and Platformer.
I collapse the setup so the three charts have more room. My game,
tags, budget and platform stay visible here.
The catalog contains 125,854 games from an August 2026 snapshot.
Prices are recorded U.S. dollar prices. They may include discounts
from that date. With these conditions, I have fifty-seven candidates.

1:15-2:25 | Why these three charts
【从左到右指 Shared tags、Reviews、Prices in USD。】
I used an UpSet-style matrix because I want to show exactly which tags
match. Each column is a tag, and each row is one exact combination.
The bar shows how many games have that combination.

The scatterplot compares positive review rate with review volume.
Higher means a higher positive rate, and farther right means more
reviews. Ninety percent from ten reviews carries different evidence
from ninety percent from thousands. Position makes both values easy
to compare, and the log axis accommodates a wide range of counts.
The directly labeled names are the most-reviewed candidates.

The price chart uses ordered dollar ranges and horizontal bars.
All bars start at zero, so I can compare the number of games in each
range. Empty ranges are hidden to give useful information more room.

2:25-3:45 | Relax a condition and follow the same games
【点 Make Platformer optional。指 57 before -> 581 now 和 +524 NEW。】
Now I'll keep Rogue-like and Indie, but make Platformer optional.
My results grow from fifty-seven to five hundred eighty-one.
Five hundred twenty-four games have entered the search.
Those NEW games are hollow diamonds. The original games are filled
circles. NEW refers to the change in this search.

【点左图 ● ● ○ 那一行的 Highlight。指三个图和选中数量。】
I'll click Highlight on the two-tag row to look more closely at that group.
The same games are highlighted in the scatterplot and the price bars.
Selected points keep their original shape and gain a stronger fill
and outline. The direct labels now follow the selected group.
The total is still five hundred eighty-one; five hundred twenty-four
are selected. The table below shows the games in that selection.

3:45-4:25 | Deal with overlap
【在 Reviews 点 Density，悬停一个格子，然后点一个格子。】
With hundreds of results, points overlap. Density groups the same
games into cells. Darker cells contain more games.
I can hover for the count, then click a cell to see those games across
the other views and in the table. This gives me an overview before
I inspect individual titles.

4:25-5:00 | Finish with a decision
【点表格里一个游戏名字，指高亮匹配标签和价格。保留详情窗结尾。】
Here I can check the matching tags, the recorded price and the review
evidence for one game. I can save it to my shortlist, compare it with
another title, and open its Steam page to check the current offer.
The dashboard is now hosted so other players can try the same search.
That is the path I wanted to support: start with a familiar game,
adjust one preference, and arrive at a few titles to investigate.
Thank you.
