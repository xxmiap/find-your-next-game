INFORMATION VISUALIZATION

How to read the charts

The three views help you find matches, compare review evidence, and see what changes with a different budget.

What changes when you click?

Filters set which games qualify. Highlights connect the same games across charts and narrow the list. NEW marks games that entered the result set relative to the chosen baseline.

With up to three search tags, each game fits just one tag row. Prices use ordered bands, and bar length counts games. On the scatterplot, vertical position shows positive rate and horizontal position shows review count on a log scale; shape identifies newly included games. In Density mode, each cell counts the games in that range. Darker cells hold more games; an outline indicates a selection. Hover a cell to see its counts, or click it to select its games.

Check the charts without color

Preview the charts in grayscale to see whether shapes, labels, and contrast still communicate the selection and the comparison.

CATALOG & COVERAGE

About the data

What the numbers mean

  • Prices are recorded in U.S. dollars as observed in the source snapshot and may reflect discounts at that time. Check Steam for today's offer.
  • The source does not distinguish free games from missing storefront prices at $0. Those entries show Unverified. They are searchable and can appear under No cap; they are excluded from specific dollar caps.
  • Matching uses recorded tags. Games with no recorded tags are still searchable; choosing manual tags lets you explore when a starting game lacks tag data.
  • Positive rate is positive reviews divided by total reviews. A game with zero reviews stays in the candidate list but has no rate to plot. Platform flags describe recorded support; check Steam for system requirements.
  • Owner ranges are SteamSpy estimates. Segments encode the order of the ranges; Details shows the source estimate.
